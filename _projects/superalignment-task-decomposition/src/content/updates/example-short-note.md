> **Example entry.** This draft exists only to check the design of short notes. It is excluded from production builds and contains no research results. Delete it, or keep it as a draft for reference.

Short notes record a step forward, a change of plan, or a result that is not yet ready for a full write-up. They usually need no headings: a few paragraphs, a link to the [longer example article](/updates/example-long-article/), and perhaps a single equation such as $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$.

State what happened, what it means for the project, and what comes next. If something did not work, say so; negative results belong in the record too.
