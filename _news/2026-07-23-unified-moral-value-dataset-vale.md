Our paper [A Unified Moral-Value Dataset for Instruction Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21279) has been accepted at the [4th International Workshop on Value Engineering in AI (VALE 2026)](https://vale2026.iiia.csic.es/), co-located with IJCAI-ECAI 2026 in Bremen. It merges existing moral-value datasets into a single instruction-tuning corpus, [available on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/teohzzh/value-for-instruction-tuning). Congratulations to Zhaohui! 🎉
