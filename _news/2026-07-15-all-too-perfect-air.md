Our paper [All too perfect: bias and aspiration in persona generation with LLMs](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10462-026-11641-3) has been published in *Artificial Intelligence Review*! We generate 40,000 synthetic personas across four languages and find that LLMs overwhelmingly produce middle-aged, aspirational, upbeat characters — a narrative sanitization that flattens representational diversity. Congratulations to Nicholas, Rafaela, David, Ana, and Julia! 🎭
