New preprint! [Data Attribution of Emergent Misalignment with Persona Features](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11025) traces the persona features behind emergent misalignment back to pre-training data, and finds that human-written documents alone do not reliably induce it — synthetic instruction-response pairs from the same content do. Great work, Clemens and David! 🧬
